# Follow-up and opportunity health

Contact the customer when an agreed date arrives, relevant new evidence appears,
requested work is complete, an authorized condition occurs, or an applicable
commercial change has been verified. Elapsed time or fear of being forgotten
does not create value.

Structure: one previously agreed fact, one new fact/completed action, one easy
next step. With no trigger, wait and record what would justify renewed contact.
Repeated weak replies prompt reassessment rather than a universal attempt count.

Respect the exact scope of a refusal. Stop rejected marketing; keep required
service, correction and delivery updates through agreed channels. Do not switch
channels to resume the same rejected request.

Assess opportunity health from a real problem, decisive facts, evaluation and
approval paths, mutual actions and completed commitments. A friendly relationship
cannot replace missing project evidence.
