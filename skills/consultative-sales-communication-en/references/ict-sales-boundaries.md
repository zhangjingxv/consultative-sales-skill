# Technical and commercial evidence boundaries

Separate customer requirements, verified facts, seller hypotheses and unknowns.
Use “awaiting pricing,” “requires technical confirmation” and “not yet confirmed.”

Exact model suffixes, part numbers, quantities and connections matter. Similar
models do not prove compatibility. Configurator acceptance proves only acceptance
by that session's rules, not price, stock, orderability, OS support or delivery.
Identify unmet requirements and missing commitments before proposing alternatives.
Marketing copy does not establish acceptance evidence.

Historical quotes are candidates, not current prices. Tax, freight, service,
payment, acceptance and delivery scope must be aligned. Recheck time-sensitive
stock, offers and lead times. “Approximately” must not become a firm quotation.

Ask the decisive missing fact instead of a complete requirements form.
Never expose supplier cost, margin, rebates, private supplier identities,
internal approvals, customer identities or private risk scores.

Formal requirements, BOMs, pricing, quotations, contracts, delivery documents,
orders, payments, signing and system submissions belong to the project workflow.
Use `ict-sales-operations` if available; otherwise describe the needed verification
and keep completion and authorization unresolved.
