# Flexible reply patterns

Adapt these structures to actual facts and the user's voice; do not copy mechanically.

- Fit: “Do you mainly integrate customer projects or supply equipment through channels?”
- Discovery: “You mentioned expansion. Is capacity or delivery timing the first constraint?”
- Price gap: “To avoid quoting the wrong scope, is this a complete system or one component?”
- Meeting invitation: state a verified overlap and offer one specific low-effort time.
  Do not request location, attendees and requirements before interest is expressed.
- Agreed meeting: confirm time, location and objective; only ask for missing logistics.
- Concern: acknowledge accurately, identify the decisive gap, then verify rather than reassure blindly.
- Follow-up: reference one agreed matter, provide one verified update and offer one next step.
  Without an update or checkpoint, wait.
- Closure: “Understood. I'll leave it there. If [real trigger] arises, send it over.”
- Call: identity, one purpose, expected duration and option to reschedule.
- Recap: confirmed facts, unknowns, agreed actions, owners and dates.
- Quick mode: decision plus one copy-ready reply; keep analysis out of customer text.
- Review: words, facts, assumptions, effective action, risk and one next action.
