# Negotiation and commitments

Before comparing price, align product/service scope, quantity, tax, delivery,
payment, acceptance and risk. Incomplete scope makes the comparison uncertain.

Clarify whether the price concern is budget, value, scope or risk. Address one
main cause; do not instantly promise a discount or disparage another supplier.

Concessions require real authority and clear changed conditions. Possible
exchanges include scope, quantity, service, payment milestones or delivery batches.
Explain the trade-off; never invent approval, a minimum price or a deadline.

“If we adjust [condition], I can check whether [concession] is available.
It is not approved yet; I'll confirm before making a commitment.”

Close with confirmed facts, unresolved issues, action owners, dates and the next
decision criterion. “In principle” is not an order or budget approval.
If there is no feasible intersection, explain the gap and exit the current option.
