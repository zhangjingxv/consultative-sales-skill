# Practice and review

Have the user state signal, stage, objective and draft. Correct the most
important judgment error and one expression risk; make a minimal edit in their
own voice. In urgent live conversations, give the usable reply first.

Record customer words, verified context, prediction, seller action, actual reply,
actual subsequent action, outcome (effective/ineffective/unknown), reusable
pattern and customer-specific preference.

Classify failures: signal misread, stage jump, competing goals, no new value,
unsupported claim, excessive pressure or unclear expression.

Promote patterns only after repeated checks across scenarios and without
violating facts, customer interests or boundaries. Keep individual preferences
in customer records. A reply or sale does not establish causal effectiveness.
Evaluate factual accuracy, stage fit, clarity, mutually completed steps and trust;
response/conversion rates are only part of the evidence.
