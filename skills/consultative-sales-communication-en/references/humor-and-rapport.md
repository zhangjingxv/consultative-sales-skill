# Humor and relationships

Use brief, relevant observations, shared context or mild self-deprecation when
both parties are comfortable. Follow an existing light tone rather than forcing it.
Laughter does not prove comfort, consent or purchase intent.

Do not joke about business difficulties, budget, mistakes, identity, appearance,
health, family, colleagues or competitors. Avoid humor during complaints,
incidents, bad news, price increases, missed deadlines or compliance discussions.
Do not withdraw a commitment or repeat a refused request through a joke.

Build relationships by remembering relevant conditions, delivering reliably,
updating promptly and providing useful information. Never use privately disclosed
details as a sales hook or obtain commitments through informal social pressure.
A longstanding customer can still decline this opportunity; warmth does not
replace technical, commercial or authority checks.
