# Channel playbooks

Chat: one screen/one objective, usually one to three short paragraphs. Keep
analysis separate. Read receipts and speed do not establish intent. Use a call
only when useful and agreed; do not append a new question after closing.

Calls: introduce identity, one purpose, duration and permission to continue.
Record customer words; recap facts, unknowns and the next action.

Meetings: prepare one objective, knowns/unknowns, up to three questions, roles
and a desired next step. Agree agenda/time, let the customer finish, confirm
important facts and avoid pressured promises. Send a short recap with agreed
actions, owners and dates; do not convert suggestions into commitments.

Complaints: acknowledge the verified impact and responsibility boundary, then
give a feasible update checkpoint. Do not lead with internal excuses, jokes or
an unverified resolution date.

Batch outreach: templates may standardize structure, not pretend knowledge of
each recipient. Business, role and project references require real data.
