# Customer feedback and deal review

Use actual customer feedback to improve the next conversation or purchasing step.
Help with the live task first, without assigning training. Practice is optional:
only when requested, let the user state signal, stage, objective and draft, then
correct one important judgment error and one expression risk in their own voice.

Keep relationship progress separate from commercial progress. Voluntary sharing
and reciprocal conversation can indicate comfort, not an order. Procurement
progress needs evidence such as confirmed requirements, completed review, an
accepted purchase request, an order or payment.

Review validated product value, confirmed need, accuracy of understanding and kept
commitments separately. Chat frequency, compliance or an absent order do not establish
the level of trust. Update understanding when the customer corrects it.

Record the purpose (ordinary contact/business), supplied facts with source/date,
verified context, prediction, seller action, actual reply and subsequent action,
observed relationship change, evidenced deal progress (or unchanged/unknown),
reusable pattern and individual preference. Update only from actual feedback;
never invent a response or imply that a record has been saved. Persist only in a
user-designated private customer record, not a public repository; see
[customer context](customer-context.md).

Classify failures: signal misread, stage jump, competing goals, repetitive sales
chasing without new value or an agreed duty, unsupported claims, excessive pressure
or unclear expression. Ordinary conversation needs neither commercial value nor
a question; when buying is ready, a missing concrete next step is an opportunity lost.

Promote patterns only after repeated checks across scenarios and without
violating facts, customer interests or boundaries. Keep individual preferences
in customer records. A reply or sale does not establish causal effectiveness.
Assess honest natural connection and respected boundaries separately from resolved
buying blockers, completed actions, orders and payments. A pleasant conversation
without a project is not a failed sale. Calculate response or conversion rates only
with a defined sample and observation period; neither alone establishes effectiveness.
