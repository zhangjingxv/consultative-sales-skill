# Self-regulation and rhythm

Separate your fear of losing a sale from a reason to contact the customer.
Valid reasons include new evidence/options, an agreed checkpoint, a customer
question, or a required delivery, correction or risk update. A genuine, welcome
topic that fits the relationship also allows ordinary contact: see
[customer context](customer-context.md). It need not include a product or question.
Otherwise wait.

Silence means no new information. It proves neither rejection nor agreement.
Do not append another prompt to soothe anxiety or finish a customer's sentence
in a meeting. Allow a pause and reassess facts.

Keep one objective and at most one entry point; ordinary conversation need not
request a response. As involvement falls, shorten, reduce
effort or stop. As it rises, clarify one further layer. Do not equate contact
frequency with relationship quality.

Before sending: is this human connection, an agreed duty or a grounded sales step;
can the customer easily decline; and do the timing and relationship fit without
smuggling a sales request into a social topic? When angry, anxious or eager to please, pause and
rewrite a factual sentence before committing to an action.
