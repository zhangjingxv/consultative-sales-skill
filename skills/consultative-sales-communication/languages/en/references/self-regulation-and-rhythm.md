# Self-regulation and rhythm

Separate your fear of losing a sale from a reason to contact the customer.
Valid reasons include new evidence/options, an agreed checkpoint, a customer
question, or a required delivery, correction or risk update. Otherwise wait.

Silence means no new information. It proves neither rejection nor agreement.
Do not append another prompt to soothe anxiety or finish a customer's sentence
in a meeting. Allow a pause and reassess facts.

Keep one objective and one entry point. As involvement falls, shorten, reduce
effort or stop. As it rises, clarify one further layer. Do not equate contact
frequency with relationship quality.

Before sending: what is new, can the customer easily decline, and what verified
opportunity is lost by waiting? When angry, anxious or eager to please, pause and
rewrite a factual sentence before committing to an action.
