# Buying roles and mutual action

A contact's support establishes only that person's action. Identify relevant
users, technical evaluators, procurement, finance/budget owners, security/legal,
final approvers and internal coordinators. One person can hold several roles;
unknown roles stay unknown.

Clarify what each role evaluates, which evidence they require, who can veto,
the sequence and the timing. Do not bypass the contact or require them to sell
internally on your behalf.

Provide a neutral forwardable summary: problem, agreed scope, candidate option,
evidence, unknowns, risks and one proposed confirmation. Personal enthusiasm,
technical approval or forwarding a document is not budget/procurement approval.

For validation or commercial steps record:

| Action | Owner | Date | Completion evidence | Blocker |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Only include agreed actions. Missing owners/dates/evidence remain tentative.
If the customer declines an introduction, respect it. If a mandatory condition
cannot be met, explain the gap. Without a mutual next step, defer the opportunity.
