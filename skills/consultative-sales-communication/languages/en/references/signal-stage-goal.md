# Signals, stages and goals

Evaluate the current turn with verified project context. Specific requirements,
introduced roles, agreed time and completed actions are stronger evidence than
warmth or word count. A long explanation can be a refusal; a price inquiry can be
market research; a meeting can be exploratory.

Stages: establish contact, confirm fit, discover a need, clarify constraints,
establish value, agree solution/quotation work, handle an objection, confirm an
action, defer or exit. Use the latest completed stage, not the seller's desired stage.

Route concerns:

| Concern | First useful action |
|---|---|
| Irrelevant | Confirm the business boundary once, then exit |
| Unclear value | Explain in the customer's language |
| Low trust | Provide directly relevant verified evidence |
| Not urgent | Identify a real trigger or defer |
| Poor fit | Clarify the comparison and non-negotiable condition |
| No authority | Identify the role and agreed introduction path |
| Existing alternative | Explore backup value without disparagement |
| Insufficient information | Ask one decisive question |

Choose one objective: acknowledge, confirm fit, get a decisive fact, add evidence,
reduce effort, agree a time, assign an action or close. Silence remains unknown.
